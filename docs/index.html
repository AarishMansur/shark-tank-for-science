Shark Tank for Science

Scouts pitch researcher and company pairs, sharks argue, a critic checks every claim against the records, and only cited proposals come out. Built on Mozaik for the EINSTEIN track.

These pages are the pitch rooms of the two committed live runs on Gemini. Press play to watch the run, or drag the timeline. Click any card for its history: who pitched it, the verdicts, and every claim the critic killed.

Main run
All 489 challenges against 1,047 researchers. 11 pairs pitched, 2 killed by dealbreakers, 9 proposals, 11 claims killed by the critic. Reverse search
A dairy describes losing yoghurt and cheese batches to milk quality swings, in plain words. 6 researchers pitched, 4 killed by the sharks with dealbreakers, 2 proposals.

Everything shown is replayed from the recordings in the repo. To run it yourself: npm install, then npm run replay (no API key needed).
