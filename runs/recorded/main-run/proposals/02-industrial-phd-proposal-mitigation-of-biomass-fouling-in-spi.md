# Industrial PhD Proposal: Mitigation of Biomass Fouling in Spiral-Wound Ultrafiltration Membranes for Bioprocess Streams

This three-year industrial PhD project tackles severe membrane fouling during fermentation broth ultrafiltration at Ferment-Pro d.o.o. by combining advanced fouling characterisation with optimised pre-treatment and cleaning-in-place protocols. Led by an experienced doctoral candidate under the supervision of Professor Ivona Bogdanović, the research bridges laboratory-scale membrane science with authentic industrial processing conditions. The collaboration targets a reduction in cleaning frequency, restoring nominal throughput and eliminating the 11% production cost penalty.

## The problem
Ferment-Pro d.o.o. suffers from severe membrane fouling during downstream ultrafiltration of fermentation broths, causing operations to run at only 55% of nominal throughput [C144-CH2]. Membranes foul within 36 hours instead of the targeted 72 hours, forcing two cleaning cycles per batch and adding 11% to total production costs [C144-CH2]. Whole cells and cell debris passing through the existing depth-filter setup contribute to this rapid fouling of the 10 kDa spiral-wound polyethersulfone membranes [C144-CH2].

## Research questions
1. How do residual whole cells and cell debris breaking through depth filters interact with 10 kDa spiral-wound polyethersulfone membranes to govern flux decline and irreversible fouling resistance?
2. To what extent can targeted enzymatic pre-treatment or modified pre-filtration mitigate biomass deposition on 10 kDa polyethersulfone membranes during fermentation broth ultrafiltration?
3. What cleaning-in-place protocol adjustments can reliably restore pure water flux and extend membrane operational life from 36 hours to the target 72 hours per cycle?

## Plan

| Year 1 | Characterise authentic industrial feed streams and fouled 10 kDa spiral-wound polyethersulfone membranes using resistance-in-series models and foulant analysis in the university laboratory. | Quantification of reversible and irreversible fouling resistances and identification of exact foulant composition from Ferment-Pro production batches. |
| Year 2 | Evaluate pre-treatment strategies, including protease pre-treatment adaptations and pre-filtration enhancements, on bench-scale cross-flow ultrafiltration rigs using authentic fermentation broths. | Validated pre-treatment protocol that increases limiting flux and prevents premature biomass accumulation on the membrane surface. |
| Year 3 | Implement and validate optimized cleaning-in-place protocols and integrated pre-treatment on industrial pilot and production lines at Ferment-Pro d.o.o. | Extended operational cycle duration from 36 hours to 72 hours, elimination of extra cleaning cycles, and verified reduction of total production costs. |
