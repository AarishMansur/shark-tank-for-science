## The problem
EkoGradnja Sistem a.d. wants to prepare an environmental product declaration for their building panels to enter the German market but needs guidance on structuring the inventory and impact categories. The company has production data but lacks the expertise to structure the inventory or categories for a full life-cycle assessment to claim an Environmental Product Declaration.

## Research questions
1. How can primary production data from wet-cast concrete panel manufacturing be systematically converted into a life-cycle inventory compliant with EN 15804 standards?
2. What is the sensitivity of global warming potential and modules A1-A3 impact categories to regional background database adaptations for Serbian raw material inputs?
3. How can an automated data-collection framework be established between ERP-based batch tracking and LCA software to streamline future EPD renewals?
